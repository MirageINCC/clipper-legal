Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Clipper is a private tool run locally on the operator's own computer. It has no users other than the operator.

Data accessed

  • YouTube / TikTok: OAuth access tokens for the operator's own accounts, used only to upload videos and read basic account info (e.g. TikTok creator info required for posting).
  • Twitch: public stream metadata and public chat messages, used to detect highlight moments. Chat user IDs in local logs are hashed.

Storage

All data and tokens are stored only on the operator's computer. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to any server other than the official YouTube, TikTok and Twitch APIs.

Retention & deletion

Tokens can be revoked at any time in the Google account / TikTok app settings and are deleted locally on request. Local logs and clips are deleted by the operator as needed.
