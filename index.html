Clipper

Clipper is a private, non-commercial tool run by a single creator on their own computer. It detects highlight moments in Twitch streams (with the streamers' permission), edits them into vertical short videos with subtitles, and uploads them to the creator's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users.
